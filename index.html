ABOUT SOUNDSPACE

Music for your mood.
Space for your emotions.

SoundSpace is a mood-based music website created for relaxation, easing tension and helping people—especially teenagers—find a moment to pause and reset.

01

The problem

Teenagers can experience pressure from school, expectations, relationships and everyday life. During stressful moments, emotions may become harder to manage.

02

The idea

SoundSpace starts with one simple question: how are you feeling? Users select an emotion and explore songs connected to that emotional direction.

03

The goal

To create a space for relaxation, calm and emotional reset through the power of music.

🎭

5 emotions

Happiness, sadness, anger, anxiety and irritation.

🎵

50 songs

Ten songs selected for each emotional direction.

✨

Why they fit

Every song includes an explanation of its emotional connection.
