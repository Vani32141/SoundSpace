THE IDEA BEHIND SOUNDSPACE

Why It Works

SoundSpace is built around a simple idea: when emotions change, the music we connect with can change too.

01

Music already speaks to us.

Music is often described as food for the soul, and for many teenagers, it is already an important part of everyday life. We listen to music when we're celebrating, thinking, relaxing, travelling, studying and simply trying to understand how we feel.

SoundSpace takes that existing connection with music and turns it into a simple starting point: begin with your emotion.

02
♫

Emotion comes first.

Instead of asking you to search through endless songs, SoundSpace begins with how you're feeling. You choose the emotion that feels closest to your current moment.

03
◌

The music follows.

Each emotion has a carefully selected collection of songs. The songs are chosen for qualities such as their rhythm, energy, atmosphere and emotional direction.

04
✦

The goal is a moment.

SoundSpace isn't about promising an instant fix. It is about creating a moment where someone can pause, listen and hopefully feel a little calmer, lighter or more understood.

THE STORY

About SoundSpace

A music-based project built from a simple question: what if finding the right music could start with how you feel?

THE BEGINNING

It started with a problem.

SoundSpace began with thinking about a problem teenagers can face every day: dealing with stress, pressure and difficult emotions.

I wanted to think beyond simply identifying the problem. I wanted to find an effective and accessible way to create a small moment of relief.

I considered creating a YouTube channel, but I wanted something that felt more focused and purposeful. I also love music, so I started asking myself what would happen if I combined those two ideas.

That question became SoundSpace: a website where people can begin with their emotion and discover music selected for that particular emotional space.

◎

WHO IT'S FOR

Made for people who love music.

SoundSpace is for anyone who connects with music, especially teenagers and people moving through stressful moments.

♫

WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT

Start with the feeling.

Rather than searching for a song first, SoundSpace puts your current emotion at the centre of the experience and lets the music follow.

✦

THE VISION

A place people remember.

The goal is for SoundSpace to become a place people can reference when they need a musical moment to help them pause, reset and move forward.

LOOKING AHEAD

I want SoundSpace to matter.

I don't want SoundSpace to simply be another website people visit once. I want it to become something people remember and return to—a place where someone can arrive in a difficult moment, choose how they feel, find a song that fits, and give themselves a little space to breathe.
